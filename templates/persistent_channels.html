Configured Channels

For accounts marked “Reserved for Fruit” in Settings, Fruit checks saved channel resolution slowly in the background: at most one channel per free account every 10 minutes. All resolution checks pause during channel or account activity and for 10 minutes afterward. It skips the check if the provider cannot confirm zero active connections.

Offline EPG Links

Fruit saves XMLTV channel IDs and names from the provider feed. This index helps find possible guide links for saved channels without programme data. Similar names are suggestions only; no guide is assigned automatically. Refreshing the index reads the provider XMLTV feed once. Regular persistent guide refreshes also update it.
