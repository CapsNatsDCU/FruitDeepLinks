Persistent Channels

Search the saved channel cache across every provider category or only categories enabled for event ingestion. Refresh Channel Cache when you need a new provider snapshot; searching and adding channels do not contact the provider catalog. Cached presence does not verify playback. The XMLTV guide refreshes when channels are saved and during the regular Xtream refresh. Provider credentials remain in deployment secrets.

Configured Channels

Resolution updates automatically when playback or a recording starts, using the existing stream. Queue test checks channels without playing them. Queued checks wait for activity to finish, then use a 10-second cooldown and a healthy free account marked “Reserved for Fruit”. Failed checks pause for two minutes.

External XMLTV / zap2xml

Add XMLTV feeds from zap2xml or another guide service. Each source keeps its own stations and refresh status. Browse one source or all sources, then choose a guide for a persistent channel. Regular guide refreshes check each source when its last check is at least six hours old.

Imported XMLTV stations

Search the imported station names or IDs, preview their schedules, and assign them to your saved persistent channels.

Offline EPG Links

Fruit saves XMLTV channel IDs and names from the provider feed. This index helps find possible guide links for saved channels without programme data. Similar names are suggestions only; no guide is assigned automatically. Refreshing the index reads the provider XMLTV feed once. Regular persistent guide refreshes also update it.

Resolution check queue

Choose Queue test on saved channels or search results. Manual requests are saved and checked in order, even after you close this page. Newly added permanent channels are checked next, newest first, ahead of channels still needing a resolution measurement. Automatic rechecks run only when there are no initial checks or missing measurements left. Successful queued checks are spaced 10 seconds apart, with a 10-second pause after activity. Playback and recordings take priority. Checks need an idle, healthy account marked “Reserved for Fruit” in Settings.
