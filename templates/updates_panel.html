    Docker Compose

    In your deployment checkout, include docker-compose.updater.yml when starting the app, then keep the host helper running:

    python3 bin/xsort_updater.py

    The complete first-time setup and recovery instructions are in docs/UPDATER.md in your checkout. Updates use your configured repository and branch. Portainer installations need a host-managed source-build Compose checkout first.
